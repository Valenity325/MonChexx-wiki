Atualizações de conteúdo

Season 2 Begins

Season 2 starts on November 4 and runs until November 30.

20 new plushies

  • 20 new plushies join the claw machines and Plushie packs. Six of them are evolutions.

New mode: Castle Defense

  • Season 2's main content. Step through the lobby's Castle Defense gate to play. See New Mode: Castle Defense for more.

A fresh season

  • Ranked points and records start over.
  • The Hall of Fame (Battles · Claw Machine) and the Castle Defense Records start fresh for Season 2.

Season 1 rewards

  • Season 1 ends on November 3.
  • Players with 5 or more Season 1 ranked matches get coins and a tier title by their final tier.
  • The top 3 on the Hall of Fame's Battles and Claw Machine boards earn a ranking title.
  • Rewards arrive when Season 2 begins.
