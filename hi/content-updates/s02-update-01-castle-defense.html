New Mode: Castle Defense

Season 2's main content. Stop the enemies pouring in through three gates and keep the toy castle standing. Step through the lobby's Castle Defense gate to play.

How a run goes

  • Every round sends enemies through all three gates at once. The deeper the round, the more enemies come, and the stronger they are.
  • Bosses and big bosses come too. Getting past the first big boss is the first clear, and the run keeps going after that.
  • The run ends when the castle falls. Your longest time holding the castle is your record for the season.
  • If you leave in the middle of a run, it is saved, and next time you go on from the start of that round.

Plushies

  • When the run starts and before each boss, 3 plushies stand in front of the castle gate. Step onto one and it joins you. The first time, only plushies with ranged attacks are offered.
  • Every plushie joins at Lv.1 and levels up during the run. Levels raised with the claw machine are not used here.
  • Plushies take their places by themselves. Walk your avatar up to an enemy and press the crosshair: your checked plushies go after that enemy only.

Augments and items

  • Clear all of a round's enemies to pick a stat augment. The augment you pick is added to every plushie until the run ends.
  • Defeat a boss to pick a special augment, and a big boss to also pick a special item, and give it to a plushie.

Stars

  • Fallen enemies drop stars. Spend them on Castle Upgrades, the Random Shop and Flags.

Play together

  • Invite friends: up to 3 players defend one castle together.
  • Solo and Together records show separately on the lobby's Castle Defense Records board.

Rewards

  • Earn coins for every round you pass, and more for boss rounds. The coins come all at once when the run ends.

Robux

  • Reroll: draw new choices for plushies, augments, items and the Random Shop. The choices you just saw do not come back. Up to 10 a run, starting at 100 R$ and rising by 100 R$ each time to 1,000 R$. You can see the odds before you draw.
  • Castle Revive: stand the fallen castle back up with 50% of its max health. Once a run, 500 R$.

See the rules and numbers in the wiki's Castle Defense tab.
