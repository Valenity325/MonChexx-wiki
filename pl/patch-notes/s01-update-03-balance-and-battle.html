MonChexx Patch Notes: Battle Tempo & Skills

Season 1 · Update 03 · October 17

Battle system

  • Attack speed now differs by doll: 0.7–1.5 attacks per second (was 1.5 for every doll, 2 for Voltis).
  • Attack speed never goes above 4 attacks per second, whatever effects add up.
  • Once sudden death starts, every doll attacks 1.5 times as fast.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75 for melee dolls and from 75 to 125 for ranged dolls. A skill now usually comes every 3 basic attacks up close and every 5 from range.
  • Stance skills (Bonis's Bone Shield, Pebbleshell's Stone Shell) now gain no Mana until the stance ends. Skills that buff or weaken start filling Mana right after they are used.
  • Taunted enemies now attack only the doll that taunted them and cannot start a new skill while taunted. A skill already started still finishes.
  • Every doll's Health is 25% lower.
  • Skill and passive coefficients are much lower. Most are now 90–500%, so far less damage, healing and shielding is wasted past a target's remaining Health.
  • Every doll was rebalanced at Lv.11.

Presentation

  • A doll using a skill glows in the skill's color and leaps, and the skill's name appears for 1 second where its level is shown, just above the health bar.
  • A doll hit by a skill flinches and shakes, and a big hit shakes the screen the way it lands. Screen shake can be turned off in Settings.
  • Basic attacks have smaller effects and damage numbers and a quieter swing. Skills are louder.
  • Damage numbers pop out to the side and fall instead of rising.
  • Doll details show attack speed (attacks per second) instead of the attack interval. A bigger number attacks faster.
  • A doll's Mana bar sparkles when it is almost full and flashes the moment it uses its skill.

Eight new evolutions (Evolution Surge)

They join the game for the first time, so these are their numbers now, not changes. Numbers given as a share of Attack, Magic Power or Max Health grow as a doll levels up.

Voidling · Tank · Range 1 cell

  • Lv.1: Health 255 · Attack 18 · Magic power 18 · Armor 30 · Magic resist 30 · Attack speed 0.8/s · Range 1 cell · Melee · Move speed 5.8 · Mana 0 / 40
  • Void Absorption: Increases own Max Health by 200% of Magic Power plus 3% of its Max Health at battle start, and restores Health by the amount gained. Then deals magic damage equal to 20% of current Health to all enemies within 1 cell of self. The added Max Health lasts until the battle ends.
  • Void Feast: Gains 10 Mana whenever an enemy within 4 cells uses an active skill.

Polarrock · Tank / Fighter · Range 1 cell

  • Lv.1: Health 360 · Attack 22 · Magic power 22 · Armor 35 · Magic resist 35 · Attack speed 0.8/s · Range 1 cell · Melee · Move speed 5.6 · Mana 0 / 75
  • Frost Slam: Deals physical damage equal to 240% of Attack to the current target and Freezes it for 1.2 s, unable to act or gain Mana. Deals 120% to other enemies within 1 cell of it and puts Frost on them, slowing their Attack Speed by 15% for 8 s.
  • Crystal Skin: Reduces magic damage taken by 20%, magic damage over time included. Applied after Magic Resist.

Honeyqueen · Support / Ranged · Range 3 cells

  • Lv.1: Health 255 · Attack 20 · Magic power 20 · Armor 10 · Magic resist 10 · Attack speed 1/s · Range 3 cells · Ranged · Move speed 5.6 · Mana 0 / 100
  • Lv.1: Bee health 60 · Bee attack 40
  • Royal Jelly: Heals all of her worker bees by 10% of their Max Health and increases their Attack Speed by 200% for 3 s. Then, if she has fewer bees than her maximum, summons 1 worker bee into an empty adjacent cell.
  • Worker Swarm: Keeps up to 3 worker bees. Their stats grow with Honeyqueen's level, and ally effects limited to a number of allies never choose them. Each bee takes magic damage equal to 4% of its Max Health every second. When Honeyqueen is defeated, her bees go berserk: the damage they deal and take is multiplied by 2.

Ironshell · Tank · Range 1 cell

  • Lv.1: Health 345 · Attack 20 · Magic power 20 · Armor 35 · Magic resist 35 · Attack speed 0.8/s · Range 1 cell · Melee · Move speed 5 · Mana 0 / 75
  • Spinning Barrage: Turns counterclockwise one direction at a time, starting from the way it faces, and fires its cannon 6 times. Each shot deals physical damage equal to 500% of Attack to the first enemy within 2 cells in that direction. Shots are 0.5 s apart, and the gap changes with Attack Speed. It does not move while firing; if Stunned or Frozen, it fires no more shots. Gains no Mana until the barrage is over.
  • Fortress Shell: Gains 35 Armor and Magic Resist for each enemy within 1 cell of self.

Bellossus · Tank / Support · Range 1 cell

  • Lv.1: Health 360 · Attack 18 · Magic power 18 · Armor 35 · Magic resist 35 · Attack speed 0.8/s · Range 1 cell · Melee · Move speed 5.4 · Mana 0 / 75
  • Toll: Deals magic damage equal to 90% of Magic Power to all enemies within 2 cells of self and taunts them for 2 s. Taunted enemies attack only this unit and can't start a skill for that time. For the same time, whenever an enemy basic attack hits it, deals magic damage equal to 25% of that hit, before armor and shields, to all enemies within 2 cells of self. Gains no Mana until the taunt is over.
  • Resonance: Whenever another ally within 2 cells of self uses an active skill, restores Health equal to 50% of Magic Power.

Moldozer · Tank / Assassin · Range 1 cell

  • Lv.1: Health 330 · Attack 14 · Magic power 14 · Armor 55 · Magic resist 25 · Attack speed 0.8/s · Range 1 cell · Melee · Move speed 6.5 · Mana 0 / 75
  • Tunnel Strike: Burrows, removing all debuffs from self, travels at 3x move speed to the empty cell with the most enemies within 1 cell of it, then deals physical damage equal to 500% of Attack to all enemies within 1 cell. Cannot be hit while burrowing.
  • Stone Claws: Gains Armor equal to 20% of Attack.

Optimouse · Ranged · Range 4 cells

  • Lv.1: Health 300 · Attack 20 · Magic power 20 · Armor 10 · Magic resist 10 · Attack speed 0.7/s · Range 4 cells · Ranged · Move speed 5.6 · Mana 0 / 300
  • Laser Cannon: Charges its laser cannon for 1.5 s toward the current target within 4 cells, then fires a laser 1 cell wide to the edge of the court, dealing magic damage equal to 1000% of Attack to every enemy in it. The direction is set when the charge begins; if Stunned or Frozen while charging, the shot is canceled.
  • Preheat Circuit: Each basic attack increases own Attack Speed by 20%, with no limit. Using its active skill resets the increase.

Candric · Support / Ranged · Range 3 cells

  • Lv.1: Health 450 · Attack 16 · Magic power 16 · Armor 10 · Magic resist 10 · Attack speed 1/s · Range 3 cells · Ranged · Move speed 5.2 · Mana 0 / 125
  • Undying Flame: Marks the ally, other than self, with the lowest current Health for 5 s. Candric also gives the marked ally a shield equal to 200% of its own Magic Power for the same duration. Whenever direct damage would defeat the marked ally, self takes 200% of that damage instead, and the ally takes none of it. Only one mark is kept, and it does not stop defeat by damage over time.
  • Melting Wax: Direct damage taken, after shields, is lost from Health little by little over 4 s. Damage over time is taken at once.
