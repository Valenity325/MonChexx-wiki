MonChexx Patch Notes: Item Drops and Graves · Serenis Adjusted

Season 2 · Update 03 · November 5

Castle Defense: item drops

  • Items are no longer picked from cards. They fall to the floor, your avatar picks them up within 1.5 cells, and you then choose which plushie gets each one.
  • Boss: Now drops 2 common items. It dropped none before.
  • Big Boss: Changes from picking 1 of 3 special items to dropping 2 common items and 1~2 special items.
  • Item Draw: Changes from picking 1 of 3 to dropping 1 item. A special item still comes out now and then.
  • Special Draw: Changes from picking 1 of 3 to dropping 1 special item.
  • An item you picked up can be discarded, and an item on the floor does not go away. Items no longer have "Reroll".

Castle Defense: play

  • A fallen plushie now leaves a grave where it fell, with the seconds until it stands again over it. It still stands again on its own cell.
  • Plushie cards and "Our Team" now always show the 2 item slots.
  • Star colors change: 1 gold · 2~4 mint · 5~9 sky blue · 10~19 pink · 20 or more white.
  • Enemies drop more stars: Elites 3 → 5 (10 from round 8) · Boss 10 → 20 · Big Boss 20 → 30.
  • Every item now shows on the plushie wearing it.
  • Decoy Totem now looks like the wooden duck on its card.
  • Undying Heart now stands on the field as a heart. Split and Summon now have effects as they go off, and Undying Heart as it beats.

Plushie changes

Serenis

  • Driving Wave: Serenis now uses a passive of its own instead of Hydris's "Surging Current". When its basic attack lands, it pushes the target 1 cell away (cooldown: 4 seconds).
