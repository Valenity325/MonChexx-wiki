MonChexx Patch Notes: Mana Spring · Stars in a Jump · Abyzel's Role

Season 2 · Update 04 · November 6

Castle Defense

  • Mana Spring: Changes from +20 to +5 mana every second.
  • While your avatar is jumping, it now picks up stars within 2 cells. On the ground it is still 1.5 cells, and items stay at 1.5 cells even while jumping.
  • Fixed messages covering the item description while a card window was open. They now appear in the window's free space.

Plushie changes

Abyzel

  • Role changes from Caster to Tank. Its stats and ranked battles stay the same.
  • In Castle Defense, its role trait changes from "Damage dealt amplified 10%" to "Damage taken reduced 20%", and special augments for a role now follow Tank.
