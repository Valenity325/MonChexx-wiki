MonChexx Patch Notes: Stages · 2× Speed · Summons

Season 2 · Update 07 · November 10

Castle Defense

  • Stages: Every 10 rounds are now a stage. Stage 1 is the town as before; from Stage 2, every stage is a different place, with its own light and weather, ground and props, and its own monsters, elites and bosses. After the last place, the places come round again from Stage 2's.
  • Bosses: Changes from one more boss every 10 rounds to a single boss, with as many elites coming in beside it as there were extra bosses (1 on rounds 15 and 20, 2 on rounds 25 and 30 …). From Stage 2, bosses and elites are the plushies of that place. Honeyqueen can now be a boss too.
  • Elites: Elites are now 1.15 times as large. From Stage 2, their health grows by 50% every stage, and the stars they drop change from 10 to 10 × the stage number (20 in Stage 2, 30 in Stage 3 …).
  • New monsters: 16 new monsters join the places (Bat · Crab · Jellyfish · Poison Frog · Scorpion · Storm Cloud · Frost Wisp · Snowman · Ice Slime · Lava Slime · Wind-up Soldier · Cog · Meteor · Spellbook · Ink Blob · Gargoyle). The Codex's Monsters and Bosses tabs are now split by stage.
  • Stat Augment: No Stat Augment is offered on a round that offers plushies.
  • Summons: Fixed Stat Augments not applying to summons. They now apply to Honeyqueen's bees, Sproutweet's tree and a Mirror Twin just as they do to plushies, and a Stat Augment picked while a summon is out applies to it at once.
  • 2× speed: A "×2" button is added beside the pause button. Press it and the battle runs twice as fast; press it again to return to normal speed. The time to pick plushies and augments and your avatar's walking stay the same. In Play together, it changes the whole run whoever presses it.
  • Castle health: The castle's health bar is larger, and its health numbers now sit right beside the bar.
