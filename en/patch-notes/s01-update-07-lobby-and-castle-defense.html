MonChexx Patch Notes: Lobby · Castle Defense · Codex

Season 1 · Update 07 · October 28

New mode: Castle Defense

  • A new mode, "Castle Defense", opens. Your plushies hold back the enemies swarming in from three gates to keep a toy castle safe, and the time the castle stands is your record.
  • Getting in: Go through the "Castle Defense" gate in the lobby. In Claw Machine & Battles, the toy castle entrance and the "Castle Defense" button on the "Build Your Team" screen take you there too.
  • Plushies: Every plushie in Castle Defense starts at Lv.1 and grows only within that run. Your collection levels are not used, and plushies you don't own can appear too. You pick your first plushie from three ranged plushies standing on pads before the castle gate, and up to 6 plushies stand at once. A fallen plushie stands up again on its cell after 10 s.
  • Rounds: Enemies come in a new round every 40 seconds. Enemy plushies lead packs of monsters such as Skeleton Soldiers, Embers, Sparks, Worker Bees and Slimes, with a boss every 5 rounds and a big boss every 10. Enemies grow stronger as rounds go by, and "Enemies level up!" shows on a round where their level rises. From the first round, the castle shoots arrows at enemies up to 4 cells away.
  • Fighting and targeting: Battles are automatic. You move about the plaza as your avatar; press the aim that appears near an enemy, and the plushies checked in "Our Team" go for that enemy.
  • Round rewards: When a round's enemies are all down, every plushie gains experience and you pick one of three Stat Augments, added to all your plushies. If enemies are still left after 40 seconds, the next round comes anyway, and the earlier round's rewards come once its enemies are all down.
  • New plushies: In round 1 and each round before a boss (4 · 9 · 14 …), three new plushies stand on the pads before the castle gate; pick one to join at Lv.1, or "Skip". With all 6 places taken, you choose which plushie to swap out.
  • Boss rewards: Defeating a boss lets you pick one of three Special Augments for one plushie; defeating a big boss also lets you pick a Special Item. Each plushie can hold up to 2 items.
  • Stars and stalls: Fallen enemies drop stars (monster 1 · elite 3 · boss 10 · big boss 20). Spend them at "Castle Upgrades" beside the castle (Castle Level: Max Health · Armor · Magic Resist · arrows), the "Random Shop" (random stats, items and special items) and "Flags" (Healing, Cheer, Frost and Fire Flags, 15 s). Stand on a stall's pad for 1 second to buy.
  • End of a run: The run ends when the castle falls or you press "Stop", and the result card shows how long the castle stood, the rounds passed and the coins earned. Passing round 10 shows "First clear!" and the run goes on. A solo run you leave midway is saved, and next time "Continue from round N" picks it up.
  • Coins: You earn 2 coins for every round passed, 4 more for a boss round, and 10 more the first time you pass each boss round. Coins are paid all at once when the run ends.
  • Play together: In the lobby and in Claw Machine & Battles, invite another player to a party with "Play together" over their avatar. A party holds up to 3 players; the first to invite becomes the leader and takes the whole party in through the Castle Defense gate. Invitations follow "Match invitations" in Settings.
  • In a party run the 6 places are shared (3 each for two players, 2 each for three), and each player picks their own augments, items and Random Shop picks, which apply only to their own plushies. Everyone gets every star a fallen enemy drops, and enemy health rises by 50% for each extra player. A gold ring at their feet shows which plushies are yours.
  • If you leave a party run midway, "Back to my castle" takes you back to your place while the run goes on. Meanwhile your plushies keep fighting on their own.
  • Records: Solo and party runs keep separate records, shown on the "Castle Defense Records" board in the lobby.
  • Reroll (Robux): Picks the current choices (Stat Augments, Special Augments, Special Items, the Random Shop's picks and the new plushies before a boss) anew from the same pool. Press and hold "Reroll" at the bottom of the screen to buy; the choices shown now are left out. Up to 10 a run, starting at 100 Robux and rising by 100 Robux each time, so the 10th costs 1,000 Robux. The rerolls used this run show as "0/10", and you can see each choice's odds before you buy. It is not available for the first plushie pick.
  • Castle Revive (Robux): When the castle falls, the battle pauses for 10 seconds, and anyone in the run can press and hold "Revive" at the bottom of the screen to stand the castle back up at 50% of its Max Health for 500 Robux. Once a run.
  • The Codex in Castle Defense adds the Monsters · Boss · Augments · Items · Traits tabs. For the full rules and numbers, see the wiki's Castle Defense tab.

Lobby

  • Every visit now starts in the lobby. New players start in the lobby too, and the tutorial begins on their first visit to Claw Machine & Battles.
  • Each mode has a gate on the lobby wall. Walk up to a gate and press "Play" on its pad to go straight to that mode; while you travel, the screen shows the mode's picture and name. This season's gate wears a "This season" band and its pad glows brightest.
  • The Claw Machine & Battles gate's screen shows a match being played in the stadium right now. With no match on, it shows the gate's picture.
  • Record boards stand beside the gates. "Hall of Fame" shows the top 10 in "Battles" (this season's rating) and "Claw Machine" (plushies caught this season), and "Castle Defense Records" the top 10 "Solo" and "Together" (the longest a castle stood). They refresh every 5 minutes.
  • The day's attendance coins now come when you enter the lobby, and the "Welcome!" card shows there too. If the day changes while you are in Claw Machine & Battles, you get them the next time you enter the lobby.
  • "Exit to Lobby" takes you back to the lobby from Claw Machine & Battles and Castle Defense. You can't go to the lobby during a match, while waiting for one, on the result screen, while setting up a friendly match or during the tutorial.

Claw Machine

  • Every claw machine is now filled at random from all the plushies in the game. At each restock, every plushie goes into each machine once before any goes in again (before: 3 kinds of plushie per machine, with only the two end machines holding every plushie).

Ranked matches & battle system

  • The 2,000 ranked opponent decks have been made anew. Each is now six plushies picked at random for its level band (before: 2,000 decks built around team compositions, plus 550 strong Lv.11 decks).
  • Shields now take damage before Armor and Magic Resist, which apply only to the damage that gets past the shield (before: the shield took the damage left after Armor and Magic Resist). Effects that increase or reduce damage dealt or taken still apply before the shield, as before.
  • Chains no longer weaken as they go. Each target of a chain takes 10% more damage or healing for every jump that actually comes after it, so the first one hit gets the most and the last gets the base amount (before: each jump 80% of the previous one). This applies to Hydris and Serenis.

Plushie changes

Flamo

  • Ember Fist: Now also explodes when a burning Bonis is defeated for the first time and revives as Bonix (before: no explosion, and the Burn was gone). It explodes only once, and the revived Bonix carries no Burn.

Hydris

  • Water Path: Instead of healing less with each jump, each target is healed 10% more for every jump that comes after it. Chained to 3 allies, it heals 120% · 110% · 100% of the heal, first to last (before: 100% · 80% · 64%).

Serenis

  • Wave Path: Instead of dealing less with each jump, each target takes 10% more damage for every jump that comes after it. Chained to 4 enemies, it deals 130% · 120% · 110% · 100% of the damage, first to last (before: 100% · 80% · 64% · 51%).

Optimouse

  • Laser Cannon: The charge time (1.5 s at base) now shortens in proportion to how much faster its Attack Speed is. Attack Speed from Preheat Circuit resets the moment the skill is used, so it does not shorten the charge.

Screens & controls

  • Codex: The Codex now opens everywhere (the lobby, Claw Machine & Battles and Castle Defense) from the book icon at the top or with K. It shows every plushie in the game this season in number order, owned or not, with each one's role, evolution, Active, Passive and Innate skills and Lv.1 stats. Plushies under maintenance are tagged "Paused".
  • The sort button beside the Codex's ✕ picks Number or Name order and roles. Close the Codex with ✕ or Q (gamepad B); while it is open, the claw stays still.
  • Skill icons: Every skill now has a painted icon, shown in the Codex, the plushie details, the skill name over the health bar and the match feed during a match, and the Castle Defense plushie pick. The icons load when the game starts.
  • PC: In Castle Defense, Space · Enter press the window's main button, Q goes back, P pauses and E presses the aim.
  • Gamepad: Open the Codex from the Top menu (⧉); LT opens its sort menu, and in Castle Defense LB · RB switch tabs. Castle Defense windows take X · A to press and B to go back, and ⧉ moves to the Top menu (pause · Codex · Settings · Exit to Lobby). The "Castle Defense" button on "Build Your Team" is X, while A still starts a match. Accept a "Play together" invitation with X · A, or pass with B.

Presentation

  • Flammonyx's "Phoenix Dive" and Voltiger's "Thunder Hunt" now leap over to behind the target instead of teleporting.
  • The lobby and Castle Defense get their own music. In Castle Defense, boss music plays while a boss is out, and a drum sounds as each round starts.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed plushies showing as 2D pictures while they loaded in a match. Plushies now show only in 3D.
