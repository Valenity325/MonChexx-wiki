Patch Notes

MonChexx Patch Notes: Tickgear Reworked · Stance Visuals

October 19

Plushie changes

Voidling

  • Role changes from Tank to Tank / Fighter.

Bellossus

  • Role changes from Tank / Support to Tank.

Tickgear

  • Max Mana changes from 100 to 60.
  • Golden Mainspring: The Mana it gains each second changes from 15 to 10.
  • Time Twist: The skill is renamed from "Time Slow" to "Time Twist". Now it deals magic damage equal to 120% of Magic Power to every enemy within 4 cells, leaving them slowed for 2 s: their Move Speed and Attack Speed drop by 50%. Every ally in the same area, self included, gets a buff that increases its Move Speed and Attack Speed by 50% for 2 s (before: magic damage equal to 430% of Magic Power to up to 3 nearest enemies within 3 cells, and their Attack Speed reduced by 50% for 3 s).

Presentation

  • Claw fingers now wear their claw's colour on the outside, so the outer side stands apart from the side that grips.
  • The Sticky Claw's colour changes from mint to fluorescent green.
  • The lobby's live screen now always shows the MonChexx logo in its top-right corner.
  • A plushie in a stance now shows it around itself for as long as the stance lasts.
  • Bonis, Bone Shield: bones circling its body
  • Pebbleshell, Stone Shell: a green turtle shell
  • Ironshell, Spinning Barrage: iron legs planted on the ground and a turntable
  • Sporoom, Spore Healing: toadstools popping up around it
  • Bellossus, Toll: a blue bell rimmed in gold

Quality of life

  • The "Bars off" button is gone from the phone battle screen. Health bars are now turned on and off with "Health bars" in Settings, as on PC.
