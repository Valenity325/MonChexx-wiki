Patch Notes

MonChexx Patch Notes: Castle Repair · Stat Augments · Presentation · Gamepad Controls

Season 2 · Update 09 · November 12

Castle Defense

  • Castle Repair: The castle now heals 10 health a second after 4 seconds without taking damage. Green crosses rise from it while it heals, and a "+" appears by its health.
  • Swapping plushies: When you swap a plushie out, the items it wore now drop on the floor around it, and you can pick them up again.
  • Pause: You can now open the Codex and Settings from the pause window too.
  • Speed Boost: Attack speed and move speed change from +8% to +5%.
  • Power Boost: Damage dealt changes from +8% to +10%.
  • When enemies grow stronger: "Enemies grow stronger!" now shows what the enemies got, each with its icon ("Sturdy: +20% health"). When a trait is added again or their level goes up, the same banner appears as that round starts.
  • Stage titles: The stage number and place name now appear larger, on a banner across the screen, for about 5 seconds.
  • Opening: "Enemies are coming!" is now "Stop the enemies!", and enemies now come out of each gate one at a time and march in single file.
  • Keyboard & gamepad: You can now turn 2× speed on and off with F (Y on a gamepad) and reopen the "Rewards" window you closed with J (RT on a gamepad).

Bug fixes

  • Fixed plushies or bosses not showing in battle when a device failed to load their look. It is now loaded again and shown.
  • Fixed Thorns and Reflect not answering the shots of Sparks, Jellyfish and Storm Clouds, which did not count as an attack.
