MonChexx Patch Notes: Balance & Battle System

Season 1 · Update 02 · October 14

Battle system

  • Damage, healing and shields of nearly every skill now scale with Attack, Magic Power or Max Health. Skills now grow stronger as a doll levels up.
  • Every doll's Health is doubled. Basic attacks still deal 100% of Attack, and skill numbers are much higher.
  • Burn and Poison now deal their damage continuously while they last, instead of once per second. Burn and Poison numbers below are per second.
  • Sudden death now starts at 20 seconds (was 25), and the draw comes at 30 seconds (was 35).
  • Dolls with a range of 2 cells or more now have more Mana: 40 → 60, 50 → 75.
  • Skills and passives sent to another doll now take effect when they arrive. Each skill travels at its own speed.
  • Dolls with full Mana now use their skill before another basic attack, so fast attackers no longer keep putting it off.
  • Every doll was rebalanced at Lv.11.

Doll changes

Bonis

  • Bone Shield: Now cast as soon as Mana is full, with no enemy needed within 1 cell. Shield changes from 10% of Max Health to 15% of Max Health + 270% of Magic Power, and Taunt from 1 to 1.5 seconds.

Bonix

  • Twin-Bone Flurry: First hit changes from 230% to 625% of Attack, second hit from 400% to 1245%.
  • Frenzied Bone: Now +1% Attack Speed for every 1% of Health lost (was every 2%), up to 80% (was 40%).

Flamo

  • Ember Fist: Damage changes from 580% to 1000% of Magic Power, Burn from 260% to 480% of Magic Power per second, and Burn duration from 4 to 2 seconds. An enemy defeated while burning from it now explodes, dealing magic damage equal to 13% of its Max Health to enemies within 2 cells.
  • Lingering Embers: Basic attacks on a burning enemy now extend Flamo's Burn to 2 seconds if less time remains. It no longer gives 25 Mana.

Flammonyx

  • Phoenix Dive: Burn changes from 115% to 245% of Magic Power per second.
  • Lingering Embers: Changed the same way as Flamo's.
  • Max Mana changes from 40 to 60.

Hydris

  • Water Path: Heal changes from 180% to 580% of Magic Power, and chain range from 1 to 2 cells. Each jump now heals 50% of the previous one.
  • Surging Current: Now pushes every enemy within 1 cell 1 cell away when Hydris takes direct damage (4-second cooldown). It used to push its target every third basic attack.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75.

Serenis

  • Wave Path: Damage changes from 140% to 620% of Magic Power, and chain range from 1 to 2 cells. Each jump now deals 50% of the previous one.
  • Surging Current: Changed the same way as Hydris's.
  • Max Mana changes from 40 to 60.

Voltis

  • Lightning Claw: Range changes from 3 to 2 cells, and damage from 600% to 1650% of Attack.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75.

Voltiger

  • Thunder Hunt: Damage changes from 620% to 2000% of Attack.

Voidren

  • Shadow Raid: Damage changes from 400% to 1450% of Attack. Bonus damage is now ×2 at 10% Health or lower (was ×1.8 at 35% or lower).
  • Void Feast: Mana gained changes from 5 to 1, and the 30-per-battle limit is removed.

Glassy

  • Crystal Burst: Range changes from 2 to 1 cell, and damage from 400% to 1230% of Magic Power on the target and from 200% to 615% on nearby enemies.

Sproutweet

  • Sunleaf Blessing: Heal changes from 150% to 620% of Magic Power.
  • Seed Shelter: The seed is now a tree on the court. Sproutweet plants it on its starting cell and steps aside. The tree has Health equal to 150% of Sproutweet's Max Health and can be broken by enemies. From 5 seconds in (was 10), it shields allies within 1 cell every 3 seconds (was once) for 4 seconds, by 210% of Sproutweet's starting Magic Power (was 160%). The tree stays when Sproutweet falls.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75.

Honeyjam

  • Honeydrop Cheer: Range changes from 2 to 4 cells, and duration from 6 to 4 seconds. It now boosts only 1 other ally, the one whose basic attacks have dealt the most damage this battle, instead of Honeyjam and the nearest ally. The Attack Speed boost stays at 100%, and meanwhile each basic attack that ally lands deals extra magic damage equal to 200% of Honeyjam's Magic Power, counted as Honeyjam's damage.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 40.

Echowing

  • Basic attack range changes from 3 to 2 cells.
  • Ear-Splitting Scream: Range and cone length change from 3 to 2 cells, and damage from 80% to 765% of Attack. It now lowers Armor by 15 for 2 seconds instead of slowing.
  • Max Mana changes from 40 to 60.

Noisewing

  • Basic attack range changes from 3 to 2 cells.
  • Hollow Scream: Range and cone length change from 3 to 2 cells, and damage from 70% to 585% of Magic Power.
  • Dissonance: The description is clearer. It works the same.
  • Max Mana changes from 40 to 60.

Pebbleshell

  • Stone Shell: Armor bonus changes from 80 to 165, and Magic Resist bonus from 60 to 125.
  • First Shell: The battle-start shield changes from 40% to 170% of Max Health.

Poisnail

  • Poison Cloud: Damage changes from 260% to 915% of Magic Power per second.
  • Toxic Slime: Poison trail damage changes from 30% to 360% of Magic Power per second.

Fluffles

  • Wing Dust: Now also deals magic damage equal to 445% of Magic Power to its target.
  • Max Mana changes from 40 to 60.

Sporoom

  • Spore Healing: Now heals every ally within 1 cell, Sporoom included, instead of up to 6 allies with the lowest Health. Heals change from 0.25, 1.25 and 2.25 seconds to 0.25, 0.5 and 1 second, and from 105% to 490% of Magic Power. Sporoom gains no Mana until the third heal is done.
  • Lingering Mycelium: Healing per second changes from 2% of each ally's Max Health to 10% of Sporoom's Max Health.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75.

Bellkin

  • Chime (new active): Replaces Silver Chime. Restores 50 Mana to every ally within 2 cells, except Bellkin.
  • Silver Bell (new passive): Replaces First Chime. When Bellkin or an ally gets a debuff, Bellkin removes all of that doll's debuffs and gains 20 Mana. It now has a 5-second cooldown instead of once per battle.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75.

Tickgear

  • Time Slow: Now deals magic damage equal to 505% of Magic Power to up to 3 nearest enemies within 3 cells and lowers their Attack Speed by 50% for 3 seconds. It used to lengthen their attack interval by 110% for 5 seconds, with no damage.
  • Tickgear no longer makes basic attacks.
  • Golden Mainspring: Mana gain changes from 10 every 6 seconds to 15 per second.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 60.

Squishpaw

  • Tunnel Ambush: Damage changes from 180% to 570% of Attack.
  • Earth Armor: Damage reduction changes from 18% to 20%.

Crystowl

  • Eye of Insight: Now targets the enemy that has taken the most damage this battle instead of the one with the highest Attack. Damage changes from 105% of Attack to 510% of Magic Power.
  • First Foresight: The direct damage bonus changes from 10% to 50%.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75.

Mouseball

  • Sawtooth Cannon: Damage changes from 165% to 670% of Attack. The shell now deals its damage when it reaches the target.
  • Max Mana changes from 50 to 75.

Melty

  • Wax Wall: Health spent changes from 7% to 20% of Max Health, and the shield from 230% to 125% of the Health spent.
  • Wax Armor: The 3-ally limit is removed.

Battle visuals

  • Skills fly from the caster and burst where they land. Hydris's and Serenis's chains now hop from doll to doll.
  • Every passive now has its own effect.
  • Gaining Mana now shows one small drop, close to the doll.
  • Flamo's burn explosion, Voidren's and Voidling's Void Feast, Voltis's lightning and Sproutweet's tree have new effects.
  • Range markers of Optimouse, Echowing and Noisewing are lighter, and chain and void skills are bolder.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed the impact of a skill not showing when it defeats its target.
  • Fixed dolls turning after a target that walks away while casting an aimed skill.
