MonChexx Patch Notes: Stat Augments · Stage Titles · Records

Season 2 · Update 08 · November 11

Castle Defense

  • Stat Augment: No Stat Augment is offered after you clear a Boss or Big Boss round; on those rounds you pick a Special Augment.
  • Stage titles: As a stage starts, its number and its place's name appear on the screen ("Stage 2" · "Night").
  • 2× speed button: The button changes from "×2" to ">>" arrows.
  • Records: The lobby's "Castle Defense Records" refreshes every 5 minutes → every 40 seconds. Your points are recorded each time you clear a round, even before the run ends, so a run in progress soon shows up on it.
