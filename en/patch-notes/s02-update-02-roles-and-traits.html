MonChexx Patch Notes: Role Rework and Role Traits

Season 2 · Update 02 · November 4

Role rework

  • Every plushie now has one role.
  • The Ranged role is removed. Plushies that had Ranged beside another role only lose Ranged. "Melee" and "Ranged" on a plushie's info still show how its basic attack works.
  • A new role, Sniper, is added. It belongs to plushies whose basic attack has a range of 4 cells or more.

Plushie changes

Flamo

  • Role changes from Fighter · Caster to Fighter.

Flammonyx

  • Basic attack range changes from 4 cells to 3 cells. This also applies to ranked battles.

Voidling

  • Role changes from Tank · Fighter to Fighter.

Glassy

  • Role changes from Tank · Caster to Tank.

Polarrock

  • Role changes from Tank · Fighter to Fighter.

Griffion

  • Role changes from Fighter · Assassin to Assassin.

Shardfin

  • Role changes from Ranged to Fighter.

Boghorn

  • Role changes from Tank · Fighter to Tank.

Echowing

  • Role changes from Support · Caster · Ranged to Caster.

Poisnail

  • Role changes from Tank · Fighter to Tank.

Frico

  • Role changes from Tank · Support to Tank.

Dreadfox

  • Role changes from Caster · Assassin to Caster.

Moldozer

Mouseball

  • Role changes from Ranged to Sniper.

Optimouse

  • Role changes from Ranged to Sniper.

Melty

  • Role changes from Tank · Support to Support.

Castle Defense: role traits

Plushies now have their role's trait from the moment they join.

  • Tank: Damage taken reduced by 20%
  • Fighter: Heals 10% of damage dealt
  • Caster: Damage dealt amplified by 10%
  • Assassin: Damage to enemies at or below 50% health amplified by 25%
  • Sniper: Range +1 cell
  • Support: +10 mana every second

Castle Defense: augments and items

  • Finishing Blow (new special augment): For Assassins. Damage to enemies at or below 50% health is amplified by 30%.
  • Farther, Harder (new special augment): For Snipers. Damage dealt is amplified by 5% for every cell to the target.
  • Mana Cycle: Now for Supports only. Its area changes from 2 cells to 6 cells.
  • Special augments for a role follow the new roles above.
  • Speed Boost: Changes from +8% attack speed to +8% attack speed and +8% move speed. "Move Boost" is removed.
  • Ring of Lightning: Each leap that reaches its target now counts as an attack.
  • Goblin Mask: Fixed an issue where enemies in the area added by the Area of Effect augment did not get its effect.

Castle Defense: play

  • Plushies are now picked from three portrait cards instead of by stepping on pads.
  • The stars of a fallen enemy now drop to the floor where it fell. They fly to your avatar once it comes within 1.5 cells.
  • Crown, Two-Handed Hammer, Thorn Armor, Vampire's Cloak and Ring of Lightning now show on the plushie's body.
  • The Castle now shows its wounds as its health falls: tears in its walls, smoke and flames. Below 25% health the edges of the screen turn red and the boss music plays.
  • "Enemies are flooding in!" now appears when a round starts with many enemies of the last round still standing.
