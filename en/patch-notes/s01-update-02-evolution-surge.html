Patch Notes

Season 1 · Update 02: Evolution Surge

Eight new evolutions arrive on October 16.

Eight new evolutions

  • Voidling: evolves from Voidren · Tank
  • Polarrock: evolves from Glassy · Tank / Fighter
  • Honeyqueen: evolves from Honeyjam · Support / Ranged
  • Ironshell: evolves from Pebbleshell · Tank
  • Bellossus: evolves from Bellkin · Tank / Support
  • Moldozer: evolves from Squishpaw · Tank / Assassin
  • Optimouse: evolves from Mouseball · Ranged
  • Candric: evolves from Melty · Support / Ranged

How to get them

  • Claw machines (event): During the event (October 16–24), every claw machine except the two at the ends of the row holds one of the new evolutions next to two other dolls. After the event, the new evolutions are stocked like every other doll.
  • Doll Exchange: Trade 10 spare dolls for the doll you pick (Pick Trade), or 3 for a random doll (Random Trade).
  • Doll packs: The new evolutions can come out of doll packs too.

Using evolved dolls

  • An evolved doll is collected on its own, and you can put it on your team as soon as you have it.
  • In Build Your Team, select a doll and tap Base or Evolution to switch its form.
  • A team can have only one doll from each evolution line.
  • An evolved doll levels up separately from its base doll.

New dolls

Numbers "of Attack", "of Magic Power" or "of Max Health" grow as the doll levels up.

Voidling · Tank · Range 1

  • Void Absorption: Max Health grows by 640% of Magic Power + 6% of its Max Health at battle start, and Voidling heals by the same amount. Then it deals magic damage equal to 10% of its current Health to all enemies within 1 cell. The extra Max Health lasts until the battle ends.
  • Void Feast: Gains 10 Mana whenever an enemy within 4 cells uses an active skill.

Polarrock · Tank/Fighter · Range 1

  • Frost Slam: Deals physical damage equal to 820% of Attack to its target and 385% to enemies within 1 cell of it, and applies Frost (−15% Attack Speed for 8 seconds). When its target has 3 Frost, it is Frozen for 1.5 seconds and can't act or gain Mana.
  • Crystal Skin: Takes 20% less magic damage, damage over time included.

Honeyqueen · Support/Ranged · Range 3

  • Royal Jelly: Heals all her worker bees by 100% of their Max Health and gives them +200% Attack Speed for 3 seconds. Summons 1 more bee if she has fewer than 3.
  • Worker Swarm: Keeps up to 3 worker bees. Their stats follow Honeyqueen's level, and each bee loses 4% of its Max Health every second. When Honeyqueen falls, her bees go berserk and deal and take double damage.

Ironshell · Tank · Range 1

  • Spinning Barrage: Spins counterclockwise in place and fires its cannon 6 times. Each shot deals physical damage equal to 1540% of Attack to the first enemy within 2 cells in that direction. It doesn't move or gain Mana while firing, and stops if Stunned or Frozen.
  • Fortress Shell: Gains 35 Armor and 35 Magic Resist for each enemy within 1 cell.

Bellossus · Tank/Support · Range 1

  • Toll: Deals magic damage equal to 555% of Magic Power to all enemies within 2 cells and Taunts them for 2 seconds. Meanwhile, 35% of each enemy basic attack it takes (before reductions) is dealt back to all enemies within 2 cells as magic damage.
  • Resonance: Heals by 120% of Magic Power whenever another ally within 2 cells uses an active skill.

Moldozer · Tank/Assassin · Range 1

  • Tunnel Strike: Removes all its debuffs and burrows to the empty cell with the most enemies around it, then deals physical damage equal to 740% of Attack to all enemies within 1 cell. It can't be hit while burrowing.
  • Stone Claws: Gains Armor equal to 50% of its Attack.
  • Starts the battle with 0 Mana.

Optimouse · Ranged · Range 4

  • Laser Cannon: Charges for 1.5 seconds, then fires a laser 1 cell wide toward its target to the edge of the court, dealing magic damage equal to 1210% of Attack to every enemy hit. The shot is canceled if it is Stunned or Frozen while charging.
  • Preheat Circuit: Each basic attack adds 15% Attack Speed, up to 100%. Using its active skill resets it.

Candric · Support/Ranged · Range 3

  • Undying Flame: Gives the ally with the lowest Health (not itself) a mark and a shield equal to 375% of Magic Power for 5 seconds. If direct damage would defeat the marked ally, Candric takes 200% of that damage instead.
  • Melting Wax: Direct damage Candric takes is lost from its Health over 4 seconds.

Ranked and badges

  • Ranked opponents use the new evolutions too.
  • The eight new evolutions don't count toward the Season 1 Collection badge.
  • The Evolution Expert badge is earned by holding 5 different evolved dolls. There are now 13 evolved dolls.
