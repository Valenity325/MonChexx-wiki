MonChexx Patch Notes: Item Draw · Special Draw · Boss Entrances · Score

Season 2 · Update 06 · November 9

Castle Defense

  • Item Draw · Special Draw: Changes from one item falling right away to picking one of 3 items. The battle pauses while you pick, and Reroll draws the three again. The item you pick falls beside the machine; your avatar picks it up and you give it to a plushie. The Item Draw still now and then (10%) offers three special items.
  • Stat Augment: The Stat Augment you pick each round no longer has Reroll. Reroll on the Stat Draw, Special Augments and plushie picks stays.
  • Boss entrances: When several bosses come in together, the camera changes from showing only the first to showing each one in turn.
  • Flag reach: Fixed a flag's reach and particles on the floor covering stars and items. They now always lie underneath.
  • Score: 40 points per round cleared → 1 point for every second the castle stands, again. Clear a round early and the points for the time left in it come in at once; a round Auto Next moved on from with enemies left gives them once those enemies have all fallen. So every round cleared still gives 40 points.
