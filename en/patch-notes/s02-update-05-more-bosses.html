MonChexx Patch Notes: Auto Next · More Bosses · Boss Health Bars · Score · Fire Flag

Season 2 · Update 05 · November 8

Castle Defense

  • Auto Next: Changes from a button you switch on (on by itself from round 21) to always on by itself from round 11. Through round 10 there is no Auto Next, and the ▶▶ Auto Next button (F on a keyboard, Y on a gamepad) is gone. As before, Auto Next never brings a boss round in early or moves on from one before it is cleared.
  • Score: Changes to 40 points for each round cleared. A round's 40 points come only once all its enemies are defeated; a round Auto Next moved on from with enemies left counts once they have all fallen. Time held in the current round no longer adds to the score. Records already set stay as they are.
  • Boss count: One more boss now comes every 10 rounds: 1 in rounds 5 and 10, 2 in rounds 15 and 20, 3 in rounds 25 and 30, and one more every 10 rounds after that. The bosses split across the gates; each extra one is a different plushie, wears items of its own and drops its own stars when it falls. Special augment cards and dropped items still come once a round, when the last boss falls.
  • "Rare" tag: The "Rare" tag on the Level Up, Area of Effect and Extra Chain cards is gone. Every special augment still comes up equally often.
  • Time left: The time until the next wave or round moves from its own panel at the top center of the screen to a small clock beside the castle health bar at the top, so it covers less of the screen.
  • Boss health bars: With several bosses, up to three now sit side by side in one row; with four or more, three show along with how many more there are ("+N"). As bosses fall, the ones left share the row again.
  • Round window: During a battle, the window now has "This round" and "Next round" tabs and opens on this round.
  • Fire Flag: Changes from 4% to 15% of max health a second as magic damage. Its minimum (15 a second) stays the same.
  • Flags: Each flag now sends up its own particles around it, and a ring of light spreads to the edge of its reach.
  • Legendary items: A legendary item on the floor now has a taller, flashier column of light and its own sounds when it lands and when you pick it up.
