MonChexx Patch Notes: Five Dolls Adjusted

Season 1 · Update 04 · October 17

Doll changes

Voidren

  • Shadow Raid: Now vanishes into the void and reappears behind its target 0.45 s later to strike. Cannot be hit while in the void.

Voidling

  • Now grows on the court with its Max Health: it shrinks to 0.7× when the battle starts, reaches 1.2× at twice its Max Health at battle start and 2× at four times, up to 3×.
  • Void Absorption: Damage changes from 20% of current Health to 10% of its own Max Health. Its range now grows to 2 cells once its Max Health reaches 3 times its Max Health at battle start, 3 cells at 4 times, and 1 more cell for each further multiple. The Max Health gain and the heal now happen the moment it casts, so that cast's damage and range already count them.

Polarrock

  • Frost Slam: Now deals damage and puts Frost on enemies in a cone spreading 2 cells behind the target (60° to each side) instead of on enemies within 1 cell of it. Damage to enemies in the cone changes from 120% to 180% of Attack, Frost lasts 6 s instead of 8 s, and the current target stays Frozen for 1.5 s instead of 1.2 s.

Bellossus

  • Toll: Now gains a shield equal to 300% of Magic Power for the 2 s the toll lasts instead of dealing damage to nearby enemies (before: magic damage equal to 90% of Magic Power to all enemies within 2 cells). Taunting enemies within 2 cells for 2 s and sending 25% of the basic attacks that hit it back at nearby enemies stay the same.

Optimouse

  • Attack speed changes from 0.7 to 0.8.

Presentation

  • Voidren is seen vanishing into the void and reappearing behind its target.
  • Voidling's Void Absorption thorns spring up ring after ring across its whole range.
  • Polarrock's Frost Slam lays a cone on the floor, with ice bursting up from every cell in it.
