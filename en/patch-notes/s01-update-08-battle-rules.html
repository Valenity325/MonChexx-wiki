MonChexx Patch Notes: Attack Rules · Skill Power

Season 1 · Update 08 · October 31

Battle rules

  • The stat "Magic power" is renamed "Skill power". Its values stay the same, and "N% of Magic Power" in skill texts now reads "N% of Skill Power".
  • All damage based on Attack is now an attack, whether a basic attack or a skill, physical damage or magic damage. Damage based on Skill Power is never an attack, whatever its kind.
  • Effects for "when an attack hits" and "hit by an attack" now work on skills based on Attack too, not only on basic attacks. They never work on an attack that is dodged or misses, nor on a target already down.
  • Effects for "each attack" and "every N attacks" count every attack, missed ones too, and once the count is reached the effect lands on the target even if that attack missed.

Plushie changes

Voltis

  • Lightning Claw: The damage type changes from magic damage to physical damage. Being based on Attack, it is now an attack.

Voltiger

  • Thunder Hunt: The damage type changes from magic damage to physical damage. Being based on Attack, it is now an attack.

Voidren

  • Shadow Raid: The damage is now based on Skill Power instead of Attack. It stays magic damage at the same ratio.

Polarrock

  • Frost Slam: The damage is now based on Skill Power instead of Attack. It stays physical damage at the same ratio.

Honeyjam

  • Honeydrop Cheer: While its buff lasts, every attack the ally lands now adds the honey damage (before: basic attacks only).
  • Hive Rhythm: The honey pot thrown every 6 attacks now bursts on the target even if that attack misses (before: only when the attack hit).

Echowing

  • Ear-Splitting Scream: Magic damage based on Attack changes to physical damage based on Skill Power, at the same ratio.

Ironshell

  • Spinning Barrage: The damage is now based on Skill Power instead of Attack. It stays physical damage at the same ratio.

Bellossus

  • Toll: Now every attack that hits it, not only basic attacks, sends part of the damage back to nearby enemies.

Moldozer

  • Tunnel Strike: The damage is now based on Skill Power instead of Attack. It stays physical damage at the same ratio.
  • Stone Claws: Changes from "increases Armor by 20% of Attack" to "every point of Armor amplifies the damage it deals by 0.2%". At 50 Armor, the damage it deals is amplified by 10%.

Mouseball

  • Sawtooth Cannon: The damage is now based on Skill Power instead of Attack. It stays physical damage at the same ratio.
