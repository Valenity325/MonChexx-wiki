MonChexx Patch Notes: Castle Protection · Play Together Rankings · Bug Fixes

Season 2 · Update 10 · November 14

Castle Defense

  • Castle protection: The castle no longer takes damage sent back by enemies' Thorns or Reflect. Its arrows can strike an enemy with Thorns without hurting the castle, and Reflect now hits only the plushies around.
  • Play together rankings: On the Play together board, a party now shares one rank, with its members' names under it. Of two parties with the very same score, the one that reached it first ranks higher. When the season ends, every member of the top 3 parties gets that rank's title.
  • Lobby: Last season's rank titles and the season news card now reach you in the lobby too.
  • Kept revive: If a Revive purchase finishes after the castle has already fallen, the Revive is kept and used for free the next time the castle falls. "Revive kept for next time" now shows when that happens.
  • Purchase window: Closing a Reroll or Revive purchase window now gives back the picking time that passed while it was open.
  • Continue: Paused or hidden plushies now leave the field when you continue a saved run.
  • Season change at midnight: A run that goes past the midnight a season ends no longer counts on the new season's board. Its coins are still paid.
  • Leaving as the castle falls: Leaving the game the moment the castle falls now settles the run as fallen.
  • Screen: Damage numbers and over-head marks now show with the camera zoomed all the way out, and a stage's weather now falls onto the field.
  • Blood Pact · Mana Link: On the same plushie, they no longer pass a full mana bar to the plushies around.
  • Die of Fate · Wind-up Mouse: They now go on from where they were after a round, a card, an item or standing back up after a fall, instead of starting from the first roll again.
  • Necklace of Thirst: "Skill power amplified 1% for every 1% of mana filled · 100% when full" changes to "Skill power amplified 100%". A skill lands after its mana has been spent, so the amplification hardly ever applied. Now it always applies, however full the bar. "Mana needed amplified 100%" stays the same.

Plushie changes

Candric

  • Undying Flame: Damage taken in an ally's place is no longer multiplied again by sudden death or by the attacker's boosts; it is 200% as described. Fixed damage bouncing endlessly between two Candrics that marked each other.

Claw machine · Friendly match

  • A try where the claw got stuck before it went down, and its head was replaced, now refunds its coins and does not count as a miss.
  • In a friendly match, changing your team after "Ready" now takes the Ready back. While a friendly match is being arranged, the claw machines are off for you.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed hit numbers, effects and sounds stopping for everyone in Play together after the host rejoined.
  • Fixed newcomers getting stuck on the team screen when a plushie the tutorial uses was Paused. The tutorial now ends and the game goes on.
  • Fixed a device's memory use growing the longer a Castle Defense run went on.
  • Fixed a reborn boss or elite (Bonix) giving no boss rewards when defeated.
  • Fixed skill effects and attack moves playing at 1x speed during 2x speed.
  • Fixed Revive not being buyable while Settings, Pause or the odds window was open.
  • Fixed continuing a run with many "Area of Effect" cards or a full field.
  • Fixed Briarowl's brambles stacking, or going unseen after it fell.
  • Fixed a party breaking up when its trip failed.
  • Fixed the danger edge and boss music staying on the result card, and the last run's numbers mixing into a retried run's tally.
  • Fixed the Twin Blades afterimage and the flag's range staying after the item was changed.
  • Fixed boards looking empty when a read briefly failed.
  • Fixed nothing showing while a Robux grant was delayed. "Your purchase is on its way" now shows.
  • Fixed a stopped match's result card not saying why, and long titles and Battle Prep notices being cut off.
  • Fixed an item's text overlapping "Swap a plushie?" when plushie cards came up while the item was still in hand.
