MonChexx Patch Notes: Pebbleshell Adjusted · Quality of Life · Bug Fix

Season 1 · Update 05 · October 18

Plushie changes

Pebbleshell

  • First Shell: The shield it gains at battle start changes from 10% to 30% of Max Health.

Quality of life

  • The prize card now closes with Space (gamepad X). It is the same key that inserts a coin at the claw machine, so pressing it once more starts the next try right away. Q (gamepad B) still closes it too.
  • "See My Plushies" on the prize card is now L (gamepad ←).
  • The Sticky Claw now comes before the Giant Claw in the claw list and in "Change claw".

Bug fixes

Flammonyx

  • Ember Siphon: In Update 02 (October 14), Flammonyx's passive was unintentionally changed along with Flamo's Lingering Embers. This is now fixed: Flammonyx has its own passive, Ember Siphon, and gains 25 Mana whenever a basic attack hits an enemy affected by Burn.
