Claw Machine

Pay coins, steer the claw and catch plushies at the arcade's claw machines. Every plushie you catch is yours, ready for Battles and Castle Defense.

How a try goes

  • Claw machines line both walls of the arcade. Open a machine's screen in front of it and press "Start playing": you pay the price of the claw you picked, and the try begins.
  • Move the claw forward, back and sideways, then press drop: the claw opens and goes down. If you do not drop it within 30 s, it drops by itself.
  • Press again on the way down to "Grab here": the claw closes at that height. It closes on what it holds, rises, and goes back over the chute to let go.
  • The claw and the plushies all move by real physics, so a plushie in the claw can slip out on the way up.
  • A plushie is yours once it is all the way inside the basket in front of the machine. One caught on the basket's rim is not yet.
  • One player uses a machine at a time. If someone is on it, you queue, and you are told when it is your turn.
  • A new player catches their first plushie in a room of their own, at one machine, without paying.

Claws

  • Each claw has its own shape and its own price per try. Switch with the claw button on the machine's screen. You pay once, when you press "Start playing", and the claw stays the same until the try is over.

Restocking

  • The machines are refilled with new plushies every 30 minutes. The arcade's screen shows the time left until the next restock.
  • Every plushie now in the game is mixed in evenly, evolved forms included as plushies of their own.

Plushies you catch

  • A plushie you catch for the first time becomes yours. Catch one you already have and it is kept as a spare.
  • Spend spares to level that plushie up: going from Lv.N to the next level takes N spares. Every level raises Health, Attack and Skill power by 15%, up to Lv.11.
  • Plushies caught after the top level are not thrown away either: they pile up as spares.

Plushie Exchange

  • At the Plushie Exchange along both side walls of the arcade, you trade spares for coins or other plushies: sell a spare for coins (1 each), trade 3 spares for a random plushie, or 10 spares for one you choose.
  • Plushies from Plushie packs cannot be sold.

Coins

  • You earn coins in Battles and Castle Defense, on your first visit of the day and by selling plushies. Your first visit of the day pays coins: 10, and members of the game's Roblox community get 10 more.
