Items

Upcoming

A plushie wears up to 2 items, common and special together, and keeps them until the run ends. Two of the same common item double its effect, but the same special item is worn only once. When it has no room, one item it wears comes off to make room, and that one is gone. Discard lets you take none.

Coming soon. Numbers and rules may still change before it opens.
