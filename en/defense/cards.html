Augments

Augments are free to pick, and the run stops while you pick.

Castle DefenseAugmentsEnemies

Stat Augment

  • Every time a round passes, pick one of 3 stat augments. The augment you pick is added to every plushie until the run ends.
  • If you leave them unpicked, they change to new augments next round.

Health Boost

All: +8% max health

Power Boost

All: +8% attack and magic power

Speed Boost

All: +8% attack speed

Guard Boost

All: +8 armor and magic resist

Mana Boost

All: +8% mana gain

Healing Boost

All: +10% healing received

Move Boost

All: +8% move speed

Special Augment

  • Defeating a boss or a big boss lets you pick one of 3 special augments. Special augments come from bosses alone.
  • Every special augment goes to one plushie: you also pick the plushie as you pick it.
  • A plushie given the same special augment again adds to it. One that a second copy would not change cannot be given to it again.
