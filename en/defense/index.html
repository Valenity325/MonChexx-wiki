Castle Defense

Stop the enemies pouring in through three gates and keep the toy castle standing. A run goes on until the castle falls, and how long you held it is your record.

Castle DefenseAugmentsItemsEnemies

How a run goes

  • A new round starts every 40 s. The next round's enemies come even if the last round's are still standing.
  • Each round brings several waves. In every wave, all three gates send out a pack of enemies at once.
  • When no enemy is left standing, the next wave comes by itself 1 s later.
  • A boss comes every 5 rounds, and a big boss every 10 rounds.
  • Getting past the big boss of round 10 is the first clear. The run keeps going after that, with no end.
  • The run ends when the castle falls. It does not end when all your plushies are down.
  • Your longest time holding the castle is your record for the season.
  • If you leave in the middle of a run, it is saved, and next time you go on from the start of that round.

Plushies

  • As round 1 and each round before a boss (4, 9, 14…) start, time stops and 3 plushies that are not on your field stand in front of the castle gate. Step onto one and it joins you.
  • The first time, only plushies with ranged attacks are offered.
  • Your field has 6 places, and that never grows. Plushies are still offered when every place is taken, and the one you pick can take the place of a plushie on your field: that one leaves with its levels, items and special augments. After the first plushie, Skip lets you take none.
  • Every plushie joins at Lv.1. Levels raised with the claw machine are not used here.
  • At the end of every round, each plushie on the field gets 1 experience. The next level takes 1 at Lv.1, 2 at Lv.2, 3 at Lv.3 and so on, so a plushie that joins late catches up quickly.
  • Each level up raises Health, Attack and Magic power by 15%.
  • A plushie that falls stands up again on its cell 10 s later.
  • Plushies take their places on the plaza by themselves, and walk back to their spot when no enemy is in sight.
  • Walk your avatar within 2 cells of an enemy and press the crosshair: your checked plushies go after that enemy only.

Stat Draw

Pick one of 3 stat augments. Depending on its tier it is several times a round's augment, and it is added to every plushie until the run ends. It never gives a special augment. It costs 120 stars, the same every time you buy it.

Flags

  • Your avatar carries the flag. Once bought, it works around your avatar for 15 s.
  • You hold one flag at a time. Buying another swaps it, and buying the same one again starts its time over.

Rewards

  • Every round you pass pays 2 coins, and a boss round 4 more.
  • The first time you pass a boss round, it pays 10 more coins, once.
  • The coins come all at once when the run ends.
