Enemies

Enemies come in packs through three gates. The deeper the round, the more of them there are and the stronger they get.

Castle DefenseAugmentsItemsEnemies

How many, how strong

  • Round 1 brings 8 enemies, and each round 4 more, up to 120.
  • The enemy level goes up by one every 4 rounds, and each level raises their Health, Attack and Magic power by 15%.
  • Enemies come in with their Mana bar 50% to 100% full.
  • An enemy fights the nearest plushie within 11 cells, and heads for the castle when none is that close.

Monsters

  • Monsters are enemies with no plushie number. They are weak but many, and each pack is all one kind.

Elites

  • From round 4, the biggest pack of every wave is led by an enemy plushie.
  • Enemy plushies are drawn from this season's plushies and fight with their level's stats and skills.

Boss

  • Every 5 rounds, a boss comes through one gate 4 s after the last wave: an enemy plushie grown big (size ×1.5, health ×2).
  • Every 10 rounds, a big boss comes instead (size ×2, health ×4).
  • Bosses and big bosses gain one more times that health every 10 rounds: ×2 from round 10, ×3 from round 20, and so on.
  • A boss wears one common item, and a big boss one special item and one common item. The round preview shows what they wear.

Traits

  • From round 8, every 4 rounds the enemies gain a trait. A trait stays until the run ends, and the same trait gained again adds up.
  • Tough: +15 armor and magic resist
  • Sturdy: +20% health
  • Fierce: +15% attack and magic power
  • Swift: +15% attack speed
  • Charged: full mana
