Battles

Build a team of 6 plushies and take on other players' teams in ranked matches. The match plays itself, and a win raises your points.

Building your team

  • Open "Build Your Team" at a team PC in the stadium's central hall. You can save several teams and pick the one to play.
  • The board is 7 cells wide and 8 cells long, and you place your plushies on any cells of your 4 rows.
  • Every plushie has a role. Where you place them decides matches: sturdy ones in front and ranged ones at the back, for example.
  • Every level raises a plushie's Health, Attack and Magic power by 15%, so higher-level plushies are stronger.
  • Once you own a plushie's evolved form, you can pick that evolution on your team, and the plushie fights in its evolved form from the start.

The match

  • The match plays itself. Plushies close in on enemies and make basic attacks, and they use their skill when their mana is full.
  • Every basic attack gives 25 mana, and every hit taken 10. After a skill, mana fills up again from 0.
  • A match lasts 30 s at most. The last 10 s are the final showdown: all damage grows by 60% every second, and plushies' attack speed is amplified 50%.
  • The match ends when all of one team's plushies have fallen. If both teams still stand when time runs out, it is a draw.
  • Every match pays coins: 3 for a win, 1 for a loss and 1 for a draw.

Points and seasons

  • Your points start at 1000, go up when you win and down when you lose. Beating an opponent with more points earns more, and you are matched with teams that have about as many points as you.
  • Each new season sets your points back to 1000. If you played at least 5 matches in a season, your points when it ends set your tier, and you get that tier's coins on your next visit.

Play with a Friend

  • From "Build Your Team" you can challenge a player on the same server. These matches do not count toward your points and pay no coins.
