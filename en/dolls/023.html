Skills

Active

Royal Jelly

Heals all of her worker bees by 10% of their Max Health and increases their Attack Speed by 200% for 3 s. Then, if she has fewer bees than her maximum, summons 1 worker bee into an empty adjacent cell.

Passive

Worker Swarm

Keeps up to 3 worker bees. Their stats grow with Honeyqueen's level, and ally effects limited to a number of allies never choose them. Each bee takes magic damage equal to 4% of its Max Health every second. When Honeyqueen is defeated, her bees go berserk: the damage they deal and take is multiplied by 2.
