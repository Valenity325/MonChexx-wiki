Active

Poison Cloud

When the current target is within 1 cell, applies Poison to enemies within 1 cell of self for 2 s, dealing magic damage equal to 915% of Magic Power per second. This Poison stacks.

Passive

Toxic Slime

When moving, leaves a poison trail on the cell it moved from for 3 s. Enemies on the trail take magic damage equal to 360% of Magic Power per second. Leaving another trail on the same cell refreshes its duration.
