Spinning Barrage

Turns counterclockwise one direction at a time, starting from the way it faces, and fires its cannon 6 times. Each shot deals physical damage equal to 500% of Attack to the first enemy within 2 cells in that direction. Shots are 0.5 s apart, and the gap changes with Attack Speed. It does not move while firing; if Stunned or Frozen, it fires no more shots. Gains no Mana until the barrage is over.
