Health, Attack and Magic power grow by 15% every level (up to Lv.11).

Active

Spore Healing

Activates at 0.25 s, 0.5 s, and 1 s after use. Each activation restores Health to every ally within 1 cell, including self, by an amount equal to 490% of Magic Power. Gains no Mana until the third activation is over.

Passive

Lingering Mycelium

On defeat, creates a mycelium field at the defeat location for 4 s. Every 1 s, the field restores Health equal to 10% of its owner's Max Health to allies within 1 cell of it.
