Active

Void Absorption

Increases own Max Health by 200% of Skill Power plus 3% of its Max Health at battle start, and restores Health by the amount gained. Then deals magic damage equal to 10% of its own Max Health to all enemies within 1 cell of self. Once its Max Health reaches 3 times its Max Health at battle start, this range grows by 1 cell, and by as much again for each further multiple. The added Max Health lasts until the battle ends.
