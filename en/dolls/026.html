Health, Attack and Magic power grow by 15% every level (up to Lv.11).

Active

Ear-Splitting Scream

Attacks a cone aimed at the current target. The cone is 2 cells long and extends 60° to each side of its center line. Deals magic damage to enemies in the cone equal to 765% of Attack and reduces their Armor by 15 for 2 s. Magic Resistance is not reduced.

Passive

Echo Mark

When the current target begins casting an active skill, applies Echo Mark. The next direct damage from an ally other than self consumes the mark and increases that damage by 25%. Applies only one mark per cast.
