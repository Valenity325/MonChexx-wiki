Health, Attack and Magic power grow by 15% every level (up to Lv.11).

Active

Laser Cannon

Charges its laser cannon for 1.5 s toward the current target within 4 cells, then fires a laser 1 cell wide to the edge of the court, dealing magic damage equal to 1210% of Attack to every enemy in it. The direction is set when the charge begins; if Stunned or Frozen while charging, the shot is canceled.

Passive

Preheat Circuit

Each basic attack increases own Attack Speed by 15%, up to a maximum increase of 100%. Using its active skill resets the increase.
