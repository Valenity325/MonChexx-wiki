Skills

Active

Laser Cannon

Charges its laser cannon for 1.5 s toward the current target within 4 cells, then fires a laser 1 cell wide to the edge of the court, dealing magic damage equal to 1000% of Attack to every enemy in it. The faster its Attack Speed, the shorter the charge, in proportion. The direction is set when the charge begins; if Stunned or Frozen while charging, the shot is canceled.

Passive

Preheat Circuit

Each basic attack increases own Attack Speed by 20%, with no limit. Using its active skill resets the increase.
