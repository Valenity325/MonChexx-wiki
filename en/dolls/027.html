Health, Attack and Magic power grow by 15% every level (up to Lv.11).

Active

Hollow Scream

Attacks a cone aimed at the current target. The cone is 2 cells long and extends 60° to each side of its center line. Deals magic damage to enemies in the cone equal to 585% of Magic Power and reduces their healing received by 40% for 4 s.

Passive

Dissonance

When the current target begins casting an active skill, delays that cast by 0.25 s. Each cast is delayed only the first time; triggering this effect again, or several at once, does not delay it further.
