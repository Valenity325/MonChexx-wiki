Passive

Seed Shelter

When the battle starts, plants a seed on its starting cell and steps back to the next cell away from the enemy. The seed takes up its cell, has Health equal to 150% of the owner's Max Health, and can be attacked and broken by enemies. After 5 s, the seed grows into a tree and grants all allies within 1 cell of it a shield equal to 210% of the owner's Magic Power at battle start for 4 s, then grants it again every 3 s. Once the tree is broken, it grants no more shields. The tree stays even if its owner has been defeated.
