Bonis

TankSeason 1

Grants itself a shield, taunts nearby enemies, and at battle start links to the other ally with the lowest Max Health in range to take part of that ally's direct damage.

Skills

Active

Bone Shield

Grants self a shield equal to 10% of Max Health plus 30% of Magic Power for 2 s. At the same time, taunts all enemies within 1 cell of self for 2 s. Taunted enemies attack only this unit and can't start a skill for that time. Gains no Mana until the taunt is over.

Passive

Bone Escort

At battle start, links to the ally with the lowest Max Health within 1 cell, excluding self. Redirects 15% of that ally's direct damage to self. The link ends when own Health is 20% or lower.

Innate

Twin-Bone Rebirth

If its owner has a Bonix plushie, the first time Bonis is defeated it revives once as Bonix after 0.5 s. The revived Bonix fights at that Bonix plushie's level, with Health at 50% of its Max Health and Mana at 0% of Max Mana. After revival, it loses 10% of Max Health each second.
