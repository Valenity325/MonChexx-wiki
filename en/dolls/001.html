Bonis

Skills

Active

Bone Shield

Grants self a shield equal to 15% of Max Health plus 270% of Magic Power for 3 s. At the same time, taunts all enemies within 1 cell of self for 1.5 s.

Passive

Bone Escort

At battle start, links to the ally with the lowest Max Health within 1 cell, excluding self. Redirects 15% of that ally's direct damage to self. The link ends when own Health is 20% or lower.

Innate

Twin-Bone Rebirth

If its owner has a Bonix doll, the first time Bonis is defeated it revives once as Bonix after 0.5 s. The revived Bonix fights at that Bonix doll's level, with Health at 50% of its Max Health and Mana at 0% of Max Mana. After revival, it loses 10% of Max Health each second.
