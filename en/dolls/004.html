Active

Phoenix Dive

Selects the enemy within 8 cells that has the most other enemies within 1 cell, then moves behind that target. Applies Burn to enemies within 1 cell of the target for 4 s, dealing magic damage equal to 245% of Magic Power per second.

Passive

Lingering Embers

When a basic attack hits an enemy affected by Burn and the Burn it applied to that enemy has less than 2 s left, extends it to 2 s.
