Content updates

Season 1 · Update 02: Evolution Surge

Eight new evolutions arrive on October 17.

Eight new evolutions

  • Voidling: evolves from Voidren · Tank / Fighter
  • Polarrock: evolves from Glassy · Tank / Fighter
  • Honeyqueen: evolves from Honeyjam · Support / Ranged
  • Ironshell: evolves from Pebbleshell · Tank
  • Bellossus: evolves from Bellkin · Tank
  • Moldozer: evolves from Squishpaw · Tank / Assassin
  • Optimouse: evolves from Mouseball · Ranged
  • Candric: evolves from Melty · Support / Ranged

How to get them

  • Claw machines (event): During the event (October 17–24), every claw machine except the two at the ends of the row holds one of the new evolutions next to two other plushies. After the event, the new evolutions are stocked like every other plushie.
  • Plushie Exchange: Trade 10 spare plushies for the plushie you pick (Pick Trade), or 3 for a random plushie (Random Trade).
  • Plushie packs: The new evolutions can come out of plushie packs too.

Using evolved plushies

  • An evolved plushie is collected on its own, and you can put it on your team as soon as you have it.
  • In Build Your Team, select a plushie and tap Base or Evolution to switch its form.
  • A team can have only one plushie from each evolution line.
  • An evolved plushie levels up separately from its base plushie.

New plushies

Numbers "of Attack", "of Magic Power" or "of Max Health" grow as the plushie levels up.

Voidling · Tank/Fighter · Range 1

  • Void Absorption: Increases own Max Health by 200% of Magic Power plus 3% of its Max Health at battle start, and restores Health by the amount gained. Then deals magic damage equal to 10% of its own Max Health to all enemies within 1 cell of self. Once its Max Health reaches 3 times its Max Health at battle start, this range grows by 1 cell, and by as much again for each further multiple. The added Max Health lasts until the battle ends.
  • Void Feast: Gains 10 Mana whenever an enemy within 4 cells uses an active skill.

Polarrock · Tank/Fighter · Range 1

  • Frost Slam: Deals physical damage equal to 240% of Attack to the current target and Freezes it for 1.5 s, unable to act or gain Mana. Deals 180% to enemies in a cone spreading 2 cells behind the target and puts Frost on them, slowing their Attack Speed by 15% for 6 s.
  • Crystal Skin: Reduces magic damage taken by 20%, magic damage over time included. Applied after Magic Resist.

Honeyqueen · Support/Ranged · Range 3

  • Royal Jelly: Heals all of her worker bees by 10% of their Max Health and increases their Attack Speed by 200% for 3 s. Then, if she has fewer bees than her maximum, summons 1 worker bee into an empty adjacent cell.
  • Worker Swarm: Keeps up to 3 worker bees. Their stats grow with Honeyqueen's level, and ally effects limited to a number of allies never choose them. Each bee takes magic damage equal to 4% of its Max Health every second. When Honeyqueen is defeated, her bees go berserk: the damage they deal and take is multiplied by 2.
  • Bee health 60 · Bee attack 40 (Lv.1)

Ironshell · Tank · Range 1

  • Spinning Barrage: Turns counterclockwise one direction at a time, starting from the way it faces, and fires its cannon 6 times. Each shot deals physical damage equal to 500% of Attack to the first enemy within 2 cells in that direction. Shots are 0.5 s apart, and the gap changes with Attack Speed. It does not move while firing; if Stunned or Frozen, it fires no more shots. Gains no Mana until the barrage is over.
  • Fortress Shell: Gains 35 Armor and Magic Resist for each enemy within 1 cell of self.

Bellossus · Tank · Range 1

  • Toll: Gains a shield equal to 300% of Magic Power and taunts all enemies within 2 cells of self, both for 2 s. Taunted enemies attack only this unit and can't start a skill for that time. For the same time, whenever an enemy basic attack hits it, deals magic damage equal to 25% of that hit, before armor and shields, to all enemies within 2 cells of self. Gains no Mana until the taunt is over.
  • Resonance: Whenever another ally within 2 cells of self uses an active skill, restores Health equal to 50% of Magic Power.

Moldozer · Tank/Assassin · Range 1

  • Tunnel Strike: Burrows, removing all debuffs from self, travels at 3x move speed to the empty cell with the most enemies within 1 cell of it, then deals physical damage equal to 500% of Attack to all enemies within 1 cell. Cannot be hit while burrowing.
  • Stone Claws: Gains Armor equal to 20% of Attack.
  • Starts the battle with 0 Mana.

Optimouse · Ranged · Range 4

  • Laser Cannon: Charges its laser cannon for 1.5 s toward the current target within 4 cells, then fires a laser 1 cell wide to the edge of the court, dealing magic damage equal to 1000% of Attack to every enemy in it. The direction is set when the charge begins; if Stunned or Frozen while charging, the shot is canceled.
  • Preheat Circuit: Each basic attack increases own Attack Speed by 20%, with no limit. Using its active skill resets the increase.

Candric · Support/Ranged · Range 3

  • Undying Flame: Marks the ally, other than self, with the lowest current Health for 5 s. Candric also gives the marked ally a shield equal to 200% of its own Magic Power for the same duration. Whenever direct damage would defeat the marked ally, self takes 200% of that damage instead, and the ally takes none of it. Only one mark is kept, and it does not stop defeat by damage over time.
  • Melting Wax: Direct damage taken, after shields, is lost from Health little by little over 4 s. Damage over time is taken at once.

Ranked and badges

  • Ranked opponents use the new evolutions too.
  • The eight new evolutions don't count toward the Season 1 Collection badge.
  • The Evolution Expert badge is earned by holding 5 different evolved plushies. There are now 13 evolved plushies.
