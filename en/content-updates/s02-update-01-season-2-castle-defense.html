Content updates

Season 2 · New Mode: Castle Defense

Season 2 starts on November 4 and runs until November 30. It brings 20 new plushies and a new mode, Castle Defense, Season 2's main content.

Season 2

  • 20 new plushies: Griffion, Cocoblin, Rocoblin, Briarowl, Shardfin, Prismlance, Lilupi, Radiant, Mudpug, Boghorn, Pumpcat, Pinehog, Frico, Abyzel, Blissfox, Dreadfox, Ashgon, Embergon, Auromarten and Obscor join the claw machines and Plushie packs.
  • Six of them are evolutions: Cocoblin → Rocoblin, Shardfin → Prismlance, Lilupi → Radiant, Mudpug → Boghorn, Blissfox → Dreadfox, Ashgon → Embergon.
  • A new season's records: ranked ratings and records start over. The Hall of Fame (Battles · Claw Machine) and the Castle Defense Records start fresh with Season 2 too.

Season 1 rewards

  • Season 1 ends on November 3.
  • Players who played at least 5 ranked matches in Season 1 get coins and a tier title for the tier they finished in.
  • The top 3 of the Hall of Fame's Battles and Claw Machine boards get a place title.
  • The rewards arrive when Season 2 starts.

New mode: Castle Defense

Stop the enemies pouring in through three gates and keep the toy castle standing. A run goes on until the castle falls, and the longer you hold it, the higher your score.

Getting in

  • Go through the "Castle Defense" gate in the lobby. In Claw Machine & Battles, the toy castle entrance and the "Castle Defense" button on the "Build Your Team" screen take you there too.

How a run goes

  • Every round sends a pack of enemies through all three gates at once. The next round's enemies come even if the last round's are still standing, and the deeper the round, the more enemies come and the stronger they are.
  • Bosses and big bosses come too. Getting past the first big boss shows "First clear!", and the run keeps going after that, with no end.
  • The run ends when the castle falls or you press "Stop". It does not end when all your plushies are down. The result card shows your score, the rounds you got past and the coins you earned.
  • If you leave a solo run in the middle, it is saved, and next time you go on from the start of that round.

Plushies

  • As the run starts and before each boss, time stops and 3 plushies not on your field stand in front of the castle gate. Step onto one and it joins you. The first time, only plushies with ranged attacks are offered; after that you can Skip.
  • Your field has 6 places, and that never grows. When every place is taken, the plushie you pick can take the place of one on your field, which leaves with its levels, items and special augments.
  • Every plushie joins at Lv.1 and gains experience at the end of every round, levelling up only within the run, so a plushie that joins late catches up quickly. Levels raised with the claw machine are not used here, and plushies you don't own show up too.
  • A plushie that falls stands up again on its cell a little later.
  • Plushies take their places on the plaza by themselves and walk back to their spot when no enemy is in sight. Walk your avatar around the plaza up to an enemy and press the crosshair: the plushies checked in "Our Team" go after that enemy only.

Augments

  • Stat Augment: clear all of a round's enemies to pick one of 3 stat augments. It is added to every plushie until the run ends. Leave it unpicked and it turns into a new one next round.
  • Special Augment: defeat a boss or a big boss to pick one of 3 special augments and give it to one plushie. The same special augment given to one plushie again adds up.
  • Augments are free, and the run pauses while you pick.

Items

  • A plushie wears up to 2 items, common and special together, and keeps them until the run ends. Two of the same common item double its effect; the same special item is worn only once.
  • When it has no room, one item it wears comes off to make room, and that one is gone. Discard lets you take none.
  • Common items come from the Random Shop's Item Draw; special items come from defeating a big boss and from the Special Draw (now and then from the Item Draw too).

Area of Effect and Extra Chain

  • The special augment "Area of Effect" makes every area of a plushie's skill, passive, items and special augments 1 cell wider, and "Extra Chain" makes its chains reach one more.
  • Items and special augments take them too: every item area, the Ring of Lightning's leap reach and leaps, the Storm Crown's reach, the flag a Flag Bearer carries, Avenge's and Mana Cycle's reach, and Chain Lightning's leap reach and targets all grow.
  • A plushie with such an item or special augment is offered Area of Effect and Extra Chain too.

Lifesteal

  • Lifesteal heals by a share of the damage dealt. Damage over time, such as Burning and Poisoned, and damage a shield blocks heal nothing.
  • The Lifesteal augment and the Berserker's Blade heal from direct blows; the Vampire's Cloak heals from all the damage dealt, its items' too.

Stars and the shops

  • Fallen enemies drop stars (a monster 1 · an enemy plushie 3 · a boss 10 · a big boss 20). A run starts with 5 stars, and a monster that blows itself up gives none.
  • Stand on a stand for a moment to buy from it.
  • Castle Upgrades: on both sides of the castle, raise the castle's level. Every level adds to the castle's most health and heals it as much, raises its Armor and Magic Resist, and makes its arrow stronger with more arrows a volley. Each level costs more.
  • Random Shop: pick one of 3 from the Stat Draw (stat augments, several times as strong by tier), the Item Draw (common items, now and then a special item) or the Special Draw (special items). The price never changes.
  • Flags: your avatar carries a flag, and for a while after you buy it the flag works within 2 cells around the avatar. You carry one flag; buying another swaps it. They unlock one after another as the rounds go on.
    • Healing Flag: plushies and summons around it heal.
    • Cheer Flag: plushies around it attack faster.
    • Frost Flag: enemies around it slow down, and enough frost freezes them.
    • Fire Flag: enemies around it keep taking magic damage.

The castle

  • The castle's health carries over from round to round and is filled only by its level. Plushies' heals and shields never reach it.
  • From the very start, the castle shoots arrows at the nearest enemy within 4 cells.

Enemies

  • Monsters: Skeleton Warriors, Skeleton Archers, Skeleton Bombers, Embers, Sparks, Worker Bees, Slimes and Poison Shrooms come in packs. Skeleton Bombers and Embers blow up, Sparks shock, Poison Shrooms poison, and a Slime splits in two smaller ones as it falls.
  • Enemy plushies: deeper in, an enemy plushie leads the biggest pack. Enemy plushies come from this season's plushies and fight with their level's stats and skills.
  • Bosses and big bosses: an enemy plushie comes out bigger. A boss wears a common item, a big boss a special item and a common one, and the round preview shows which. Their health grows the deeper the round.
  • Traits: deeper in, the enemies gain a trait now and then, and it stays until the run ends. A round where the enemies' level rises shows "Enemies level up!".

Play together

  • In the lobby and in Claw Machine & Battles, invite another player with "Play together" over their avatar. A party holds up to 3, and the player who first invited leads it, taking the party through the Castle Defense gate together. Invitations follow the Settings' "Match invitations".
  • The 6 places on the field are shared out (3 each for two players, 2 each for three), and each player picks their own augments, items and draws, which apply only to their own plushies. Everyone gets all the stars from fallen enemies, and the enemies' health grows by 50% for every extra player. Your plushies wear a gold ring at their feet.
  • If you leave a run played together, "Back to my castle" takes you back to your places while the run goes on. Meanwhile your plushies fight by themselves.

Records and rewards

  • Your score goes up with every second you hold the castle. Get past a round early by defeating all its enemies, and the time it had left goes into your score at once.
  • Solo and Together records (the season's best score) are kept apart, and the lobby's Castle Defense Records board shows the top 10 of each.
  • Earn coins for every round you get past, more for a boss round, and more again the first time you get past each boss round. The coins arrive all at once when the run ends.

Robux

  • Reroll: draws the offer you are choosing from again, from the same pool: new plushies, stat augments, special augments, special items and the Random Shop's draws. Hold "Reroll" at the bottom of the screen to buy it; the offer you see now does not come back.
  • Up to 10 a run, from 100 R$, 100 R$ more every time, 1,000 R$ for the 10th. The count shows as "0/10", and before you buy you can see the odds of every candidate.
  • Castle Revive: when the castle falls, the battle pauses for a moment, and anyone in the run can hold "Revive" at the bottom of the screen to stand the castle up again at 50% of its most health for 500 R$. Once a run.

Controls and screens

  • Codex: in Castle Defense, the Codex adds the Monsters · Boss · Augments · Items · Traits tabs.
  • PC: Space · Enter press the window's main button, Q goes back, P pauses and E presses the crosshair; hold R to buy the Reroll or Revive at the bottom of the screen.
  • Gamepad: windows take X · A to press and B to go back, and ⧉ moves to the Top menu (pause · Codex · Settings · Exit to Lobby). In the Codex, LB · RB switch tabs. Hold Y to Reroll and X to Revive, and LB shows the odds. The "Castle Defense" button on "Build Your Team" is X. Accept a "Play together" invitation with X · A, or pass with B.
  • Music: Castle Defense has its own music. Boss music plays while a boss is out, and a drum sounds as each round starts.
