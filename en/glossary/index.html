Glossary

The effects and words you meet in battle. In skill texts and posts, point at a word with a dotted underline, or tap it, to see a short explanation.

Buffs

Buff

Any helpful effect on a plushie.

Effects that raise Attack Speed or Armor, for example. Effects that remove debuffs never remove buffs.

Related plushiesTickgear

Shield

A barrier that takes damage before Health does.

It blocks physical, magic and over-time damage alike and fades when its time is up. A shield takes damage before Armor and Magic Resist reduce it. Armor and Magic Resist apply only to the damage that gets past the shield. Shields from different skills add up; the same skill shielding again replaces its own.

Related plushiesBonisSproutweetPebbleshellBellossusMeltyCandric

Guarded

Protected by another plushie's mark.

When direct damage would make it fall, it takes none and the plushie that marked it takes the hit instead. It is not saved from falling to damage over time.

Related plushiesCandric

Reflecting

When struck by an attack, sends part of that blow back to nearby enemies as magic damage.

Damage that is not an attack, such as damage based on Skill Power or damage over time, is not sent back. The amount comes from the blow before Armor and shields reduce it, and an attack a helm blocks is sent back too, from the blow before the helm stopped it. Thorns send the blow back to the attacker alone, as physical damage.

Related plushiesBellossus

Thorns

When struck by an attack, sends part of that blow back to the attacker as physical damage.

Like reflect, but it goes to the attacker alone, not to nearby enemies. Damage that is not an attack, such as damage based on Skill Power or damage over time, is not sent back. The amount comes from the blow before Armor and shields reduce it, and an attack a helm blocks is sent back too, from the blow before the helm stopped it.

Void

Gone from the court for a moment, so nothing can hit it.

Damage over time does not land meanwhile either, and its debuffs stay on.

Related plushiesVoidren

Burrowed

Travelling underground, where nothing can hit it.

Going under clears all its debuffs, and damage over time does not land while it travels.

Related plushiesSquishpawMoldozer

Reviving

Down for a moment before getting back up.

While it waits nothing can target it, and it still counts as standing, so its team cannot lose yet.

Related plushiesBonis

Berserk

Deals and takes more damage.

When its owner falls, a summon still standing goes berserk until it falls too.

Related plushiesHoneyqueen

Heal

Restores lost Health.

It cannot go past Max Health; anything more is lost. A plushie with Healing cut heals less.

Related plushiesHydrisVoidlingSproutweetHoneyqueenSporoomBellossus

Lifesteal

Heals by a share of the damage dealt.

Damage over time, such as Burning and Poisoned, and damage a Shield blocks heal nothing. A plushie with Healing Reduction heals less.

Debuffs

Debuff

Any harmful effect on a plushie.

Effects that lower its stats or stop it from acting. An effect that removes debuffs clears them all at once and leaves buffs and shields alone.

Related plushiesFlufflesBellkinSquishpawMoldozer

Burning

Takes magic damage every second (damage per second) for a set time, 4 s by default; with several burns on, only the strongest deals damage.

A burn lasts 4 s unless the skill or item that applies it names another time. Its damage per second comes from that skill or item and is fixed when it lands. When the same effect of the same plushie applies it again, the burn already on stays and the new one is added beside it, each keeping its own time. With several burns on, only the one with the most damage per second deals damage; when it ends, the next strongest takes over. It is damage over time, so it fills no Mana.

Related plushiesFlamoFlammonyx

Poisoned

Poison deals magic damage every second (damage per second) for a set time, 6 s by default, and every poison adds up.

A poison lasts 6 s unless the skill or item that applies it names another time. Its damage per second comes from that skill or item and is fixed when it lands. Each new dose adds another poison that lasts its own time, and the damage of every poison still on is added together. It is damage over time, so it fills no Mana.

Related plushiesPoisnail

Bleeding

Takes physical damage every second (damage per second) for a set time, 4 s by default, and that much again at once each time it moves or acts.

A bleed lasts 4 s unless the skill or item that applies it names another time. Its damage per second comes from that skill or item and is fixed when it lands. When the same effect of the same plushie applies it again, the bleed already on stays and the new one is added beside it, each keeping its own time. With several bleeds on, only the one with the most damage per second deals damage; when it ends, the next strongest takes over. Each time the bleeding plushie makes a basic attack, steps to another cell, is pushed or pulled, or uses its active skill, it takes its damage per second again at once. That damage is the bleed's damage over time too: it fills no Mana, and effects that react to direct damage or to an attack do not go off. It is physical damage, so Armor reduces it.

Shocked

Takes more physical damage.

Magic damage is unchanged. The same skill applying it again starts the time over.

Related plushiesVoltis

Frost

Lowers Attack Speed.

Move speed is unchanged. Frost from standing near a chilling plushie ends as soon as it moves away; frost from a skill lasts its set time.

Related plushiesGlassyPolarrock

Slowed

Attacks and moves more slowly.

Each new layer slows it further, and each layer runs out on its own.

Related plushiesFlufflesTickgear

Healing cut

Heals it receives restore less.

Applying it again starts the time over; it never goes deeper.

Related plushiesNoisewing

Crowd control

Stunned

Cannot move, attack or use a skill.

Mana still fills. Stunned again while stunned, it stays stunned until the later end.

Related plushiesIronshellOptimouse

Frozen

Frozen solid: cannot move, attack, use a skill or gain Mana.

Frozen again while frozen, it stays frozen until the later end. Being frozen while a skill is getting ready can cancel it.

Related plushiesPolarrockIronshellOptimouse

Taunted

Attacks only the plushie that taunted it and cannot start a skill meanwhile.

Its Mana keeps filling, so it uses its skill once the taunt ends. The taunt ends at once if the taunter falls.

Related plushiesBonisBellossus

First target

When the battle starts, nearby enemies go for this plushie first.

Unlike a taunt it has no set time and does not stop skills. An enemy picks again once it has to walk.

Related plushiesPebbleshell

Damage

Physical damage

Damage that Armor reduces.

Most basic attacks deal physical damage. The more Armor, the less it takes: with 100 Armor, half.

Related plushiesBonixVoltisVoltigerPolarrockEchowingPebbleshellIronshellSquishpawMoldozerMouseball

Magic damage

Damage that Magic Resist reduces.

Damage over time such as Burning and Poisoned is magic damage too. With 100 Magic Resist it takes half, and some magic damage is based on Attack.

Direct damage

Damage a basic attack or a skill deals in one blow.

The plushie hit gains 10 Mana. Many passives react only to direct damage.

Related plushiesBonisHydrisSerenisEchowingFlufflesCrystowlCandric

Damage over time

Damage that lands bit by bit over time, like Burning and Poisoned.

It fills no Mana, and effects that react only to direct damage ignore it.

Related plushiesPolarrockFlufflesCrystowlCandric

Execute

Deals extra damage to an enemy low on Health.

It is judged by the target's Health when the blow lands, and it does not defeat on its own.

Related plushiesVoidren

Chain

An effect that jumps from the one it hit to the nearest next one.

It never hits the same one twice, and the more jumps still follow one, the more it gets. If the next one falls before the jump arrives, it moves on to the one after.

Related plushiesHydrisSerenis

Stats

Health

At 0, the plushie falls.

Max Health grows by 15% every level. Health percentage is how much of its Max Health it has left.

Attack

How much a basic attack deals, and what "N% of Attack" skills are based on.

All damage based on Attack is an attack, even a skill's and even magic damage. It grows by 15% every level.

Related plushiesBonixVoltisVoltigerPebbleshellSquishpawOptimouse

Skill power

What "N% of Skill Power" skills are based on, whether they deal physical or magic damage.

Damage based on Skill Power is never an attack. It grows by 15% every level.

Armor

Reduces physical damage taken.

With 100 Armor, physical damage is halved. It stays the same at every level.

Related plushiesEchowingPebbleshellIronshellBellossusMoldozerCrystowlMelty

Magic resist

Reduces magic damage taken.

With 100 Magic Resist, magic damage is halved. It stays the same at every level.

Related plushiesPolarrockEchowingPebbleshellIronshellCrystowlMelty

Attack speed

How many basic attacks it makes in a second.

However many effects speed it up, it never goes past 4 a second. It stays the same at every level.

Related plushiesBonixVoltigerGlassyPolarrockHoneyjamHoneyqueenIronshellFlufflesTickgearOptimouse

Move speed

How fast it walks from cell to cell.

While walking it does nothing else.

Related plushiesFlufflesTickgearSquishpawMoldozer

Range

How far a basic attack reaches: 1 cell is melee, 2 or more is ranged.

A plushie walks toward its target until it is in range. Plushies in between never block it. How far a skill reaches is written in its own text.

Related plushiesHydrisVoidlingGlassyMouseball

Mana

When it is full, the plushie uses its active skill.

It gains 25 with every basic attack and 10 whenever it takes direct damage. Using the skill empties it. While Frozen, or while a skill says it gains no Mana, nothing fills it.

Related plushiesBonisFlammonyxVoidrenVoidlingPolarrockFlufflesBellkinTickgearSquishpaw

Areas and positions

Cell

One hexagon of the court. Distances and areas are counted in cells.

The distance between two cells is how many cells you cross to get there. The court is 7 cells across and 8 cells long, 4 rows to a team. One plushie stands on a cell.

Area

The cells an effect reaches. "Within N cells" means every cell up to N cells from the center.

"Within 1 cell" is the six cells around it. Whether the one in the middle is included depends on the skill, and the area is checked the moment the effect lands.

Cone

An area that fans out from one point in one direction.

Its length is counted in cells and its width is the angle it opens to each side of its center line. Where it starts and which way it opens depend on the skill.

Related plushiesPolarrockEchowingNoisewing

Beam

An area that runs straight ahead to the end of the court.

Its direction is set when it starts getting ready, so stepping aside meanwhile dodges it.

Related plushiesOptimouse

Behind the target

The open cell next to the target that is farthest from the one moving.

Skills that move behind a target go to this cell and strike from there.

Related plushiesFlammonyxVoltigerVoidrenSquishpaw

Push

Moves enemies away from it, cell by cell.

If the cell an enemy would land on is taken or off the court, that enemy is not pushed. A pushed plushie loses the step it was taking.

Related plushiesHydrisSerenis

Field

A patch of ground that affects what is around it for a set time.

It lasts its full time even after the plushie that made it has fallen.

Related plushiesSporoom

Trail

Left on the cells a plushie leaves, hurting enemies standing there.

It is damage over time, so it fills no Mana, and it does not poison. Laying it again on the same cell starts the time over, and it stays even after the plushie that left it has fallen.

Related plushiesPoisnail

Battle rules

Attacks

Basic attacks and all damage based on Attack.

A skill's damage based on Attack is an attack too, whether physical damage or magic damage; damage based on Skill Power is never an attack, whatever its kind. An enemy's attack can be dodged or blocked by a helm, and can miss in black smoke; thorns and reflect send back part of an attack's damage. Effects for "when an attack hits", "hit by an attack" and "enemies hit by attacks" also work on an attack a helm blocks or a shield takes, but never on one that is dodged or misses, nor on a target already down. Effects for "each attack" and "every N attacks" count every attack, missed or dodged ones too, and once the count is reached the effect lands on the target even if that attack missed. Damage over time such as Burning, Poisoned and fields is not an attack, and a summon's attacks never count as its owner's.

Related plushiesFlamoFlammonyxHoneyjamBellossusOptimouse

Basic attack

The attack a plushie keeps making on a target in range, at its Attack Speed.

Each one fills 25 Mana. Most deal physical damage equal to Attack. A skill based on Attack is an attack too, but not a basic attack, so it fills no Mana. Effects that change the swing (striking again, leaping to the next enemy, spreading around) and effects set on the next blow come with basic attacks only.

Related plushiesVoltisVoltigerHoneyjamPebbleshellTickgearMouseball

Active skill

The skill a plushie uses when its Mana is full.

Using it empties the Mana, which must fill again before the next one. A taunted plushie cannot start one.

Related plushiesVoltisVoltigerVoidrenVoidlingEchowingNoisewingBellossusCrystowlOptimouse

Stance

A posture a plushie holds while using a skill; it gains no Mana meanwhile.

Until the stance ends, neither basic attacks nor damage taken fill its Mana. The skill's text says when it ends, as in "Gains no Mana until the taunt is over." Some stances also stop basic attacks, and since a stance is neither a buff nor a debuff, nothing removes it.

Related plushiesBonisPebbleshellIronshellSporoomBellossus

Passive

An ability that is always on, or works by itself at set moments.

An evolved plushie without a passive of its own uses the one it evolved from.

Innate

A special ability a plushie has through its evolution line.

Some need another plushie in your collection to work.

Current target

The enemy a plushie is attacking right now.

When it has to walk, it picks the nearest enemy again. A taunted plushie's target is the one that taunted it.

Stack

The same effect applied more than once.

What happens depends on the effect. Most just start their time over when applied again; those that pile up say so in their text.

Related plushiesGlassyPoisnailFluffles

Cooldown

How long it waits after working before it can work again.

Related plushiesHydrisSerenisBellkin

Mark

A sign a skill leaves on a plushie; what it does depends on the skill.

A mark on an enemy makes the next damage it takes bigger; a mark on an ally protects that ally.

Related plushiesEchowingCandric

Summon

Something a plushie calls up or makes and puts on the court.

It fights at its owner's level but does not count for winning or losing, and it never gains Mana. Ally effects with a limit on how many they reach skip it.

Related plushiesSproutweetHoneyqueen

Final showdown

In the last 10 s of a match, all damage grows every second and attacks speed up.

From 20 s, damage taken grows by 60% every second, and attack speed is amplified 50%.

Draw

If both teams still have a plushie after 30 s, it is a draw however much Health is left.

It is also a draw if both teams' last plushies fall at the same moment. Summons left standing do not count.

Roles

Tank

Sturdy plushies that stand in front and take the enemy's blows.

Fighter

Close-range plushies that keep dealing damage.

Caster

Plushies whose skills deal big damage or big effects.

Assassin

Plushies that slip behind the enemy to go for the weak.

Ranged

Plushies with a range of 2 cells or more that attack from afar.

Support

Plushies that heal, protect or help their allies.

Growth and seasons

Level

A plushie's rank, which goes up when you get the same plushie again.

Health, Attack and Skill Power grow by 15% every level, up to Lv.11. Armor, Magic Resist, Attack Speed, Range and Mana stay the same.

Related plushiesBonisHoneyqueen

Evolution

The next stage of a plushie.

If you have the evolved plushie, you can send it out in place of the one it evolves from when you set your team. It fights with its own stats and skills, and a team can field only one plushie from each evolution line.

Season

A stretch of time that brings new plushies, with its own leaderboard.
